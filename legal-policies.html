5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations are handled based on the type of service, time of notice, and provider terms. Common guidelines:

  • Cancellation before service begins may be eligible for a partial refund, depending on advance payment and provider policy
  • Cancellation after work has started or after materials are arranged may not be refundable
  • For urgent or same-day bookings, no refund may be available once the provider is assigned or mobilized
  • For property listings, room rentals, and marketplace products, any refund or booking adjustment is subject to the agreement between the listing party and customer
  • For customized event or decoration work, cancellations may be charged for design preparation, travel, or material procurement already done

Refund decisions are made after reviewing the booking details, communication logs, provider’s confirmation, and any actual incurred costs. Call4All may assist in dispute resolution but is not always obligated to enforce a refund if the provider has already performed the agreed service.
